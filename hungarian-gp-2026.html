Hungarian GP 2026: Norris Historic Win on Special Anniversary Day

Lando Norris claims his first victory as F1 world champion on July 26 - exactly 17 years after Lewis Hamilton's first win as champion on the same date

By F1wow Team 5 min read

A historic day at the Hungaroring as Lando Norris secured his first Formula 1 victory as a world champion, completing a remarkable circle that began exactly 17 years earlier. The McLaren driver delivered a masterful performance in changeable conditions, starting from pole position and managing a chaotic race to claim a landmark victory on a truly significant date.

📅

The July 26 Connection

This race created an extraordinary statistical anomaly that has Formula 1 fans buzzing:

  • July 26, 2009: Lewis Hamilton wins the Hungarian Grand Prix - his first victory as a Formula 1 World Champion
  • July 26, 2026: Lando Norris wins the Hungarian Grand Prix - his first victory as a Formula 1 World Champion

Both drivers achieved their breakthrough wins on the exact same calendar date at the same circuit, separated by exactly 17 years. This remarkable coincidence highlights the Hungaroring's status as a venue where careers are defined and champions are crowned.

Stat credit: @f1guydan

🏁

Race Report

Qualifying

Lando Norris claimed pole position for McLaren with a stunning lap in qualifying, beating Max Verstappen (Red Bull) and Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) to the top spot. The tight and twisty nature of the Hungaroring suited the McLaren package perfectly.

The Start

Norris made a clean start from pole, fending off an early challenge from Verstappen into Turn 1. The field negotiated the opening lap without major incidents, setting up what would become a strategic race.

Early Race Stages

The first stint saw Norris managing his tires while keeping Verstappen at bay. The Dutch driver applied immense pressure but couldn't find a way past. Ferrari's Charles Leclerc ran third but struggled with tire degradation.

The Turning Point

The race's defining moment came on lap 23 when a sudden rain shower hit sectors 2 and 3 of the circuit. Teams scrambled to react, with Norris making the perfect call to switch to intermediate tires at the optimal moment.

Final Stages

In the drying conditions, Norris showcased exceptional car control and race management. He extended his lead to over 20 seconds, crossing the line to an emotional celebration on the McLaren radio and in the pits.

Championship Impact

This victory significantly impacts the 2026 championship battle:

  • Lando Norris closes the gap to the championship leader
  • McLaren strengthens their position in the Constructors' Championship
  • Max Verstappen extends his championship lead despite second place
  • Ferrari consolidates third in the constructors' standings

Technical Notes

Tire Strategy

Most teams started on the soft compound before switching to intermediates during the rain shower, then back to softs for the final stint. McLaren's tire management was exemplary throughout.

DRS Usage

The Hungaroring's limited overtaking opportunities meant DRS activations were fewer than at most circuits, making track position even more critical.

Fuel Consumption

High ambient temperatures pushed cooling systems to the limit, with several teams reporting elevated temperatures throughout the race.

📚

Historical Context

The Hungaroring has a history of producing landmark victories:

  • 1987: Nelson Piquet wins the inaugural Hungarian GP
  • 2009: Lewis Hamilton's first win as World Champion
  • 2015: Sebastian Vettel's first win with Ferrari
  • 2026: Lando Norris' first win as World Champion

The circuit's tight layout and limited overtaking opportunities mean that qualifying performance and strategy often determine the race outcome, making it a true test of a driver's complete skillset.

What's Next

The F1 circus now heads to Belgium for the iconic Spa-Francorchamps circuit, where weather conditions often play a decisive role. Norris will carry massive momentum into the summer break.
